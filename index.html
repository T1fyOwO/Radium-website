What's in the menu

Every option lives in one tabbed window with four tabs: Global, Player, Unlocks and Replay.

  • 60 – 10000

    TPS bypass

    Change the game's ticks per second with a slider, an exact input or one-tap presets.

  • 0.01x – 10x

    Speedhack

    Slow the game down for tricky sections or speed it up. One button returns it to 1x.

  • Player tab

    Noclip

    Survive collisions, with a counter of how many deaths it blocked.

  • Unlocks tab

    Unlock icons

    Unlock every icon and color in the game.

  • Unlocks tab

    Main level bypass

    Play the main levels that are still locked on your account.

  • .radium1

    Radium Replay

    Record your inputs and play them back later. Replays are saved as .radium1 files.

Radium Replay

A built-in recorder for your inputs. Open the Replay tab inside a level to use it.

  1. Press RecordThe level restarts and your inputs are recorded from the first frame.
  2. Stop or finish the levelPress Stop, or finish the level, and the replay is saved as a .radium1 file.
  3. Play it backPick a saved replay and press Play. Radium sets the TPS the replay was recorded at and restarts the level.

Current limits

  • A recording is a single attempt. Dying restarts it from frame 0.
  • Practice mode and checkpoints are not supported yet.

Replays are saved in the mod's save folder, in a folder called replays.

Opening the menu

The way in depends on what you play on.

Windows and macOS

Tab

Press Tab to show or hide the menu. Tab is ignored while you are typing in one of its text fields.

Android

Pause a level and tap the Radium logo. Drag it to move it; it remembers where you left it. Close the menu with the X in its title bar. While the menu is open, the game ignores your touches.

Get Radium

Radium is a Geode mod, so you need Geode installed first.

  • Geode 6.0.0-beta.2 with Geometry Dash 2.2091.
  • Windows, macOS or Android 64-bit. Android 32-bit and iOS are not supported.
  • Download the .geode file for your platform from the GitHub repository and put it in Geode's mods folder.
